01

Chal Amarnath

Making a government pilgrimage-safety campaign feel like devotion, not a public notice.

Client Shri Amarnath Ji Shrine Board / Government of J&K Type Public Awareness Campaign
₹75LDeal, pitch to signed
~$90KUSD equivalent

Objective — Communicate Yatra safety and logistics information without losing the emotional and spiritual weight of the pilgrimage itself.

Concept — Paired practical safety messaging with emotional storytelling, anchored by a dedicated Yatra anthem built to give the campaign its own emotional identity — something pilgrims would carry with them, not just hear once.

Execution — Mass-reach radio commercials for safety and awareness; a dedicated pilgrimage anthem; tourism-facing communication ahead of the Yatra season.

My Role

Sold and led the campaign end-to-end — from initial pitch to the Shrine Board through to signed deal and delivery.

02

Minute Maid — "Squeeze The Real Taste"

Turning a product reveal into a live, unscripted moment of surprise.

Client Minute Maid (Coca-Cola) Platform Red FM 91.9, Srinagar Type Experiential Brand Activation

Concept — RJ Rayees performed as a live "juice man," running a real orange-juicing demo for crowds before revealing Minute Maid Pulpy Orange as the twist — turning a routine product placement into a shared moment of surprise.

Execution — RJ-led live juicing demos at Srinagar's highest-footfall locations (University of Kashmir, Polo View, City Mall). Five parallel crowd-engagement formats — Citrus Quest scavenger hunt, Orange Squeeze Relay, Orange & Spoon Race, Orange Peel Art Corner, Minute Maid Balloon Burst. A dedicated branded studio shift linked the venue to the Red FM 91.9 studio live. Full digital video capture for social amplification.

My Role

Conceived the full activation — RJ-as-vendor framing, all five game formats, hotspot selection, and the studio tie-in — and produced it end-to-end.

03

"Akhar Duggar De" — Language. Literature. Legacy.

Turning a heritage-preservation brief into a festival young audiences actually wanted to attend.

Client Own IP, produced by Red FM 93.5, Jammu Type Cultural Festival / Original IP

Concept — Transformed the festival venue into an immersive "Duggar Village" — a living recreation of regional culture, not a stage-and-stalls event.

Zones — Duggar Bazaar (artisan marketplace), Duggar Food Street (Kaladi & Ambal, Rajma & Khatta Meat), Akhar Corner (book stalls, author interactions), Art & Heritage Zone (live artisan demos), RJ Chaupal (trivia, folk games).

Main Stage — Welcome ceremony → Dogri dance showcase → literature sessions → poetry mehfil → stand-up comedy → "Pehchaan Duggar Di" attire showcase → Duggar Samman felicitation → Lifetime Achievement Award → celebrity headline performance.

Amplification — 15-day on-air campaign; 3-phase digital rollout; city-wide hoardings, posters, digital screens, and college outreach.

My Role

Built the full festival concept, on-air and digital campaign architecture, and secured talent and city-wide on-ground placement.

04

"Ayye Sunta Hai Kya?"

Listening first. Advocating second.

Client Self-initiated original IP Type Storytelling-led Social Impact Campaign

Objective — Give voice to sex workers in India through empathy-led storytelling rather than sensationalism.

Structure — Phase 1, "Ahh Se Wah Tak": an unheard-voices podcast series. Phase 2, "Bandh Darwazo Ke Peeche": digital documentary films shot inside real spaces. Phase 3, "Ab Palang Nahi, Zanjeer Tootegi": an action phase — NGO collaboration on skill development and livelihood pathways.

Scope — Kamathipura (Mumbai), Sonagachi (Kolkata), GB Road (Delhi), Budhwar Peth (Pune), Itwari (Nagpur).

My Role

Originated the concept, the three-phase structure, and the multi-city scope; wrote all campaign messaging and narrative framing.

Explaining B2B SaaS through a cup of chai going cold.
