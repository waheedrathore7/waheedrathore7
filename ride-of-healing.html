The Challenge

Plan and fund a solo, multi-day winter motorcycle expedition across the Himalayas — with no existing audience, no budget, and no prior brand relationships.

The route ran Jammu → Dharamkot → Bir → Jibhi (Sajwar) → Manali, crossing the Atal Tunnel in harsh winter conditions down to –10°C — solo riding, solo trekking, and stretches of forest road at night with no network and no one else around.

"I didn't want to travel for free. I wanted to earn my travel — by giving something more valuable than money: a story."
The Strategy

A story, not a discount ask.

Every property was pitched with a narrative hook, not a request. Instead of promotional posts, I created cinematic reels showcasing the vibe of each stay, posted real-time stories so travelers planning New Year trips could discover them instantly, and wove each stay naturally into the journey's narrative — rest, reflection, mountain mornings — rather than treating it as an ad placement.

Collaborated with hospitality properties including Tara Van, Rubaroo, and The Bunker across the route.
